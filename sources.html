📚 Data Sources & Methodology

All carbon footprint values in CarbonLib are approximate averages based on peer-reviewed literature and reputable datasets. Actual emissions vary significantly by region, supply chain, behaviour, and measurement methodology. We err toward transparency — if you spot an error or have a better source, please open a GitHub issue.

🌍 Primary datasets & research

Poore & Nemecek (2018) — Food lifecycle analysis "Reducing food's environmental impacts through producers and consumers." Science, 360(6392), 987–992. The most comprehensive food LCA study to date — basis for most food values. doi:10.1126/science.aaq0216
Our World in Data — Food & Agriculture Excellent aggregation of food production emissions data, drawing on Poore & Nemecek and others. ourworldindata.org
IPCC Sixth Assessment Report (AR6), 2021–2022 Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change — the authoritative source for global warming potentials, carbon budgets, and sector emissions. ipcc.ch
UK Government GHG Conversion Factors (DESNZ, 2023) Widely used for electricity, transport, and material lifecycle values. gov.uk
EPA Greenhouse Gas Equivalencies Calculator US EPA tool for translating CO₂ values into tangible equivalents. epa.gov

✈️ Travel emissions

ICAO Carbon Emissions Calculator International Civil Aviation Organization — flight emissions methodology including radiative forcing. icao.int
Our World in Data — Travel & Transport Per-km emissions by transport mode, based on EEA and UK government data. ourworldindata.org
NZ Ministry for the Environment — Transport Emissions New Zealand-specific vehicle emissions data. environment.govt.nz

⚡ Electricity & energy

Electricity Authority NZ / EMI Portal Grid intensity data for New Zealand electricity. emi.ea.govt.nz
IEA World Energy Outlook 2023 International Energy Agency — global energy system data and projections. iea.org
electricityMap.org Real-time and historical electricity carbon intensity by country. electricitymap.org

📱 Consumer goods & digital

Apple Environmental Progress Report (2023) Lifecycle emissions for Apple devices. apple.com/environment
Freitag et al. (2021) — Internet & Streaming emissions "The real climate and transformative potential of ICT." Patterns. doi:10.1016/j.patter.2021.100340
Cambridge Bitcoin Electricity Consumption Index (CBECI) Per-transaction energy and carbon for Bitcoin. ccaf.io

🌿 Nature & sequestration

NZ Emissions Trading Scheme Carbon sequestration rates for NZ forests. epa.govt.nz
Duarte et al. (2017) — Blue carbon Kelp and seagrass carbon sequestration rates. Nature Geoscience.
Project Drawdown Ranked climate solutions and their carbon impact. drawdown.org
⚠️ Important disclaimer: Carbon footprint values are inherently uncertain and represent averages across many different contexts. A beef steak in NZ has different emissions than one in Brazil. A kWh in NZ is much cleaner than one in Australia or Poland. These figures are intended to give useful comparative guidance — not to be used as precise accounting values. For carbon reporting purposes, please use jurisdiction-specific and activity-specific conversion factors.
